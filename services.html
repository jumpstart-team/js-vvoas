What we treat

Most patients reach us on a referral from their dentist or orthodontist. These are the procedures we perform.

Extractions and wisdom teeth

Wisdom teeth

Third molars, impacted, angled, or still under bone.

Extractions

We remove teeth that are broken, beyond restoring, or crowding the arch.

Exposure and bonding

For a tooth that has not come through on its own. We uncover it and bond a bracket to it, so your orthodontist can draw it into position.

Restoring what is missing

Dental implants

A titanium fixture set into the jaw to carry a crown, bridge or denture.

Bone grafting

The ridge thins when a tooth has been missing a while, leaving an implant nothing to hold. Grafting rebuilds it.

Sinus lift

We add height beneath the sinus floor in the upper back jaw so we can place an implant safely.

Pain, infection and diagnosis

Tooth pain or infection

We find the source and treat it, abscesses and non-vital teeth included.

Oral pathology

We examine and biopsy sores, lumps and patches that have not healed on their own.

Emergency treatment

We handle swelling, broken teeth, bleeding, and anything that goes wrong after surgery.

Facial trauma

Referred injuries limited to the jaw. A broader facial or head injury belongs in the emergency room.

Jaw surgery

Corrective surgery to reposition the jaw for bite, breathing or growth problems.

Your anesthesia

Your surgeon selects the anesthetic to suit you and the procedure. Dr. Awalt serves as an Anesthesia Evaluator for the Maryland State Board of Dental Examiners and inspects other practices on the state's behalf.

Local anesthetic

We numb the area. You stay fully awake, and you drive yourself home afterward.

Oral conscious sedation

You take medication before the appointment. It leaves you calm and drowsy, awake enough to respond, and you will remember little of the visit.

General anesthesia

Your surgeon administers it here in the office and you sleep through the procedure. Plan for a pre-operative consultation, nothing by mouth after midnight, and an adult to drive you home.

How it goes

  1. 1

    Call or send the form

    Tell the front desk what is happening and whether a dentist referred you. They will book you a time.

  2. 2

    Consultation and a plan

    Your surgeon examines you, takes films if they are needed, and explains your options, your anesthesia and what it will cost.

  3. 3

    The procedure

    We operate, send you home with written instructions, and your surgeon calls to check on you.
