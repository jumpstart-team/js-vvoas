The surgeons

Dr. Awalt built this practice on trust, communication and compassion. She explains every option plainly and puts safety first. Dr. Charnowitz joined her on those terms.

Bethany Serafin Awalt, DMD

Owner and oral & maxillofacial surgeon

Dr. Awalt studied biology as an undergraduate, graduating magna cum laude, then took her dental degree at the University of Pittsburgh School of Dental Medicine. Her hospital residency in oral and maxillofacial surgery was at Woodhull Medical Center in New York.

She has practiced for more than twenty years and teaches as a clinical instructor. She has operated on surgical missions abroad and in volunteer programs closer to home. Her day-to-day work is extractions, dental implants, bone grafting, oral pathology and outpatient anesthesia.

  • Diplomate, American Board of Oral and Maxillofacial Surgery
  • Anesthesia Evaluator Maryland State Board of Dental Examiners
  • Hospital privileges Sinai Hospital of Baltimore
  • A Baltimore Magazine Top Dentist for many years running

Neil Charnowitz, DDS

Oral & maxillofacial surgeon

Dr. Charnowitz was born and raised in Woodmere, New York. He earned his degree in biology from Yeshiva University and his dental degree from the University of Maryland School of Dentistry, where he was inducted into several honor societies. He completed his residency in oral and maxillofacial surgery at Broward Health Medical Center, affiliated with Nova Southeastern University, in Fort Lauderdale, and served as chief resident in his final year.

His scope covers wisdom tooth removal, implant placement, bone grafting and sinus augmentation, surgical extractions, oral pathology, facial trauma and corrective jaw surgery, with IV sedation and general anesthesia administered in the office. He holds on-call attending appointments at Sinai Hospital, LifeBridge Health and Johns Hopkins Howard County Medical Center, teaches as Dean’s Faculty in the Department of Oral and Maxillofacial Surgery at the University of Maryland School of Dentistry, and lectures on implant surgery, sinus augmentation, facial trauma, anesthesia safety and maxillofacial infections.

Outside it he plays volleyball and pickleball, swims, and is married to Gabby, with whom he has four children: JJ, Lilly, Cooper and Asher. His neckties are a matter of record.

  • Diplomate, American Board of Oral and Maxillofacial Surgery (ABOMS)
  • Fellow, American Association of Oral and Maxillofacial Surgeons (AAOMS)
  • Dean’s Faculty University of Maryland School of Dentistry
  • On-call attending Sinai Hospital, LifeBridge Health
  • On-call attending Johns Hopkins Howard County Medical Center
  • Member, Maryland Society of Oral and Maxillofacial Surgeons (MSOMS)
  • Member, Mid-Atlantic Society of Oral and Maxillofacial Surgeons (MASOMS)
  • Member, Maryland State Dental Association (MSDA)
  • Member, American Dental Association (ADA)
