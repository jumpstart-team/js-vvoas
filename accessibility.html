Accessibility

Last updated 2026. Our approach to making this website usable for as many visitors as possible.

Our approach

This site is built with semantic HTML, keyboard-navigable menus and forms, visible focus states, and text colors checked against the WCAG AA contrast standard. Images carry descriptive alt text, and the layout adapts down to small phone screens.

Ongoing work

Accessibility is not a one-time fix. We review this site as it changes and welcome reports of anything that does not work well with a keyboard, screen reader, or magnification.

Feedback

If you run into an accessibility barrier anywhere on this site, call the office at (410) 581-9008 or write to us at 2 Reservoir Circle, Suite 103, Pikesville, MD 21208, and we will do our best to address it.
